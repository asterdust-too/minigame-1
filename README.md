# Minigame 1
## Devlog
### Answer to prompt
  While creating a platforming level for my first minigame, I found through practice the relationship between Components, GameObjects, and Scenes as I fiddled with the level and used it to create my own small platformer. The way I think about these parts of a game and how they relate to each other, furniture comes to mind, among other things. 

  The Scene is the couch. It's what you use and what you see directly. The GameObjects are what make it up. The cushions, armrests, and feet. They make the couch what it is, and without them, it isn't a couch anymore; simply a bunch of parts. The Components are what make the GameObjects function. The pegs, leather, springs, nuts, and screws that hold everything together. If the couch didn't have these, it would fall apart immediately. Not even parts anymore, but raw materials. The Components make everything work. This way, I can understand how everything works, and know what to look for if something isn't working.
### Process
- I copied and pasted blocks to change areas of the original level to ways that made sense to me.
- Changed the Player Speed to 6 and Sprint Speed to 7. Rotation Speed was changed to 2 and Speed Change Rate to 5 for smoother turning.
- I added parkour challenges that test the player's maneuverability and control, including an area tucked away in the lower part of the map with lots of coins.
- Removed a block to show the player where to go next, which is a hard jump back onto the main area. The jump cannot be made from the other side. (think neo jumps in minecraft)
- Destroyed a small land tower and made it into a staircase.
- The staircase leads to a glass bridge game where the player must guess which grass slabs have collision or not. Leads to a small dirt island with coins.

Parkour!
